What V3.1 can handle

      
    
        
  • CSV with date, description, amount, type, category, person.
    • 
        
  • Excel .xls and .xlsx sheets with Date, Description/Narration, Debit, Credit, Withdrawal, Deposit or Amount.
    • 
        
  • Text-based PDFs with separate Withdrawal/Deposit/Balance columns; Withdrawal is the outgoing amount, Deposit is income, and Balance is never counted as spending.
    • 
        
  • Automatic mapping for known merchants; credits/reversals never count as spending. Every genuine bank withdrawal is counted in monthly outflow, while the category still identifies transfers, settlements, investments, etc.
    • 
      

      
If a PDF is scanned as images, it needs OCR and will not parse automatically in this version.
